# DeFeet Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeFeet (“DeFeet”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you interact with the DeFeet Discord bot.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on how DeFeet is configured and used, we may process:

- Discord user IDs;
- Discord server (guild) IDs;
- channel IDs;
- commands and interactions sent to the Bot;
- timestamps;
- bot preferences and settings;
- moderation and access-control information;
- technical logs, errors, and diagnostic information;
- usage statistics, such as commands used and image requests; and
- information you voluntarily provide to us.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and improve DeFeet.

## 2. Information We Do Not Need

DeFeet does not require your real name, home address, or telephone number merely to provide ordinary bot functionality.

We do not intentionally collect passwords or Discord authentication credentials.

Never send passwords or other highly sensitive information to the Bot.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may process information to:

- respond to Discord commands;
- provide requested images and other bot functionality;
- remember server or user preferences;
- enforce rate limits and prevent abuse;
- moderate the Service;
- diagnose errors and technical problems;
- maintain security;
- understand aggregate usage;
- improve DeFeet; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 4. Discord

DeFeet operates through Discord. When you use the Bot, Discord separately processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

DeFeet is an independent service and is not operated by or endorsed by Discord unless expressly stated otherwise.

## 5. Image Requests

When you request an image, we may temporarily or persistently record information such as your Discord user ID, the command used, the requested category or person, the image returned, and the time of the request where this is necessary for functionality, security, moderation, analytics, or abuse prevention.

We recommend avoiding permanent command-level histories unless they are actually required to operate DeFeet.

## 6. Cookies

The Discord bot itself does not use traditional browser cookies.

If DeFeet operates a website, dashboard, or other web service, that service may use cookies or similar technologies. Any such use should be described through the relevant website privacy notice or an updated version of this Policy.

## 7. Sharing of Information

We may disclose limited information to service providers that help us operate DeFeet, such as hosting, database, logging, security, or infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

- comply with applicable law or a valid legal request;
- investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents;
- protect the rights or safety of users or third parties; or
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including security, abuse prevention, operational, and legal requirements.

Where practical, temporary technical logs should be automatically deleted after a defined retention period.

The actual retention periods used by DeFeet should be documented internally and reflected here where appropriate.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by DeFeet.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below.

We may need to verify that the Discord account or information involved belongs to you before fulfilling certain requests.

## 11. Images of Public Figures

The DeFeet image database may contain photographs depicting celebrities, influencers, and other public figures.

If you are depicted in an image and wish to raise a privacy or removal concern, contact us with sufficient information to identify the image.

Copyright and other rights-holder requests may also be submitted through our content removal process.

## 12. Children's Privacy

DeFeet is not intended to collect personal information from children contrary to applicable law.

We do not knowingly maintain sexualized imagery of minors. If we become aware of prohibited content involving a minor, we may remove it and take other appropriate action.

## 13. International Processing

Our infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Where required, we will take reasonable measures to address applicable requirements concerning international transfers of personal information.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, features, service providers, or legal obligations change.

The effective date above indicates when this version took effect.
