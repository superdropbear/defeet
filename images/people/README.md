# CC0 real-person image intake

This directory currently uses only images indexed by Openverse as CC0 1.0.
Attribution is not legally required, but source and licence records are kept in
`sources.json` for auditability.

Acceptance rules:

- the source metadata must describe a woman/female subject;
- no child/minor terms or ambiguous child context;
- no face, name, or other identifying feature;
- non-explicit, non-sexual presentation;
- feet must be visible and useful as an art reference; and
- the individual asset must be marked CC0 1.0 on its source record.

CC0 waives copyright-related rights; it does not waive privacy, publicity,
trademark, or other third-party rights. Cropped, anonymous subjects reduce that
risk but do not eliminate it. Do not add identifiable people without a suitable
model release and verified adult status.
